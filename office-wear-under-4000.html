5 Office Days, 5 Looks: In ₹4,000

Updated November 2026

Need a new outfit every day but don't have the budget for lots of clothes? A few pieces, mixed and matched, can make 5 different looks for 5 days.

The colour rule

Keep neutral colours (navy, grey, white, black) as the base and take 1-2 accent colours (like mustard or maroon). That way every top goes with every bottom.

What to buy

  • 3 formal kurtis (solid or light print)
  • 2 bottoms: one palazzo and one straight trousers
  • 1 handbag that fits a laptop or diary
  • 1 pair of flats or comfortable sandals

The 5-day plan

DayLook
MondayKurti 1 + trousers
TuesdayKurti 2 + palazzo
WednesdayKurti 3 + trousers
ThursdayKurti 1 + palazzo
FridayKurti 2 + trousers, with a stole or scarf for a new look

The ₹4,000 budget

ItemBudget
3 formal kurtis₹1,800
2 bottoms₹900
Handbag₹800
Flats₹500
Total₹4,000

This is an estimate. You can shift the budget between items to suit your needs.

Common mistakes

  • Buying without checking the office dress code.
  • Buying 5 pieces in one colour, which cuts down your mix-and-match options.
  • Not checking the bag size. If a laptop or diary won't fit, the bag is useless.

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear a kurti to the office every day?

It depends on your office dress code. Ask HR or a senior once before you buy.

How many clothes are enough for 5 days?

3 tops and 2 bottoms give you 5 different combinations.

What kind of bag should I buy?

One that fits a laptop or diary, lunch and small items, and is comfortable on the shoulder.
