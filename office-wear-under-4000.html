Office ke 5 din, 5 look: ₹4,000 mein

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Roz naya outfit chahiye par bahut saare kapde kharidne ka budget nahi? Kuch hi cheezon ko mix-match karke 5 din ke 5 alag look ban sakte hain.

Rang ka rule

Neutral rang (navy, grey, safed, kala) ko base rakho aur 1-2 rang accent ke liye lo (jaise mustard ya maroon). Isse har top har bottom ke saath chal jaata hai.

Kya-kya kharidna hai

  • 3 formal kurtis (solid ya halka print)
  • 2 bottoms: ek palazzo aur ek straight trousers
  • 1 handbag jisme laptop ya diary aa jaye
  • 1 flats ya aaram wali sandal

Aam galtiyan

  • Office ka dress code dekhe bina kharidna.
