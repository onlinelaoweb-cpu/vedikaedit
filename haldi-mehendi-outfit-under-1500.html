Haldi and Mehendi Outfit Under ₹1,500: Colours and Fabrics

Updated November 2026

Haldi and mehendi are daytime, colourful and a bit messy functions. The right outfit is bright, light and something you won't worry about getting stained.

This guide helps you pick a good one within ₹1,500 and shows what to check before ordering.

Colours that suit each function

  • Haldi: Yellow is the classic. Orange, peach and mustard also work. Avoid white and very pale colours, since turmeric stains show.
  • Mehendi: Green, parrot green, pink and teal suit the mood. Prints and light work look good in photos.
  • Both: If you're attending both, buy two sets in different colours rather than one expensive piece.

Fabric and fit

  • Cotton or cotton blend: Breathable and easy to wash, which is ideal for functions where things get splashed.
  • Printed over embroidered: Prints hide marks better and cost less than heavy work.
  • Easy to sit in: You'll sit on the floor or low seats. Choose a set that doesn't ride up or feel tight when seated.
  • Dupatta: Light and easy to manage. A very long dupatta gets in the way and picks up stains.

Before you order

Check the size chart, whether the dupatta is included, and customer photos for the real colour. Yellow in particular can look quite different from the listing photo.

Common mistakes

  • Wearing white or very pale colours to a haldi.
  • Choosing a heavy outfit that is tiring in the daytime heat.
  • Not checking the colour in customer photos, since yellow varies a lot.

Frequently asked questions

What colour is best for haldi?

Yellow is traditional. Orange, peach and mustard also work. Avoid white, since turmeric stains easily.

Can I reuse the outfit later?

Yes, a plain cotton set in a solid colour can be worn again to casual functions.

Should I buy floral jewellery?

It's optional. It looks lovely in photos but is only for the day. Light, simple jewellery works too.
