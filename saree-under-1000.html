₹1,000 ke andar saree kaise chunein

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

₹1,000 mein bhi achhi saree mil jaati hai, bas fabric aur blouse piece ke baare mein saaf jaankari honi chahiye.

Fabric ki pehchan

  • Cotton: Roz ke liye, garmi mein aaramdayak, drape karna aasaan.
  • Georgette: Halka aur behta hua, party ke liye achha. Fisalan wala hota hai, to pehli baar ke liye thoda mushkil.
  • Chiffon: Bahut halka aur transparent, dhyan se dhona padta hai.
  • Art silk ya silk blend: Chamak wali, function ke liye. Asli silk is price mein nahi milti.

Blouse piece

Listing mein 'with blouse piece' likha ho to dekho ki blouse ka kapda alag mil raha hai ya nahi. Blouse silwane ka kharcha alag aata hai, to budget mein uska hissa rakho.

Pehli baar saree pehen rahi ho to

  • Cotton ya silk blend se shuru karo, wo sambhalna aasan hai.
  • Ready-to-wear (pre-stitched) saree bhi milti hai, jo pehenna aasan hota hai.
  • Pehle ghar par ek baar pehen ke practice kar lo.
