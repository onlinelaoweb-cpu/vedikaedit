A Festive Look in ₹1,500: Diwali and Family Functions

Updated November 2026

Want to look good at the festival without putting your whole budget into the outfit? One outfit and 2 small pieces make a full festive look.

The colour plan

Keep to one colour palette for the festival. For example, if the outfit is maroon, use gold and one soft colour in the dupatta or jewellery. Too many colours together feel heavy.

The order to buy in

  • Choose the outfit first: Pick everything else to match its colour.
  • Keep jewellery small: If the outfit has embroidery or zari, keep the jewellery light.
  • Take the dupatta in a contrast colour: It's the cheapest way to change the look.
  • Pick footwear that will work again: One you can wear with other outfits too.

This is an estimate. You can shift the budget between items to suit your needs.

Common mistakes

  • Buying everything new. An older dupatta or jewellery can make the look too.
  • Ordering right before the festival, when delivery can run late.
  • Wearing too many colours at once.

Frequently asked questions

Can I put together a festive look in ₹1,500?

Yes, a good kurti or kurta set, jhumkas and a dupatta can fit in ₹1,500.

How much jewellery should I wear?

Small if the outfit is heavy, one statement piece if it's simple.

Which colours look good?

Maroon, bottle green, mustard and royal blue stand out at festivals.
