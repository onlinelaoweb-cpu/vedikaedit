Palazzo, Leggings or Pants for Kurti: What Goes With What

Updated November 2026

The bottom changes the whole look of a kurti. The same kurti can look casual, formal or festive depending on what you pair it with.

Here is a simple guide to match bottoms to kurti length and occasion.

Match by kurti length

  • Short kurti (above the knee): Leggings, jeans or straight pants. Palazzo can look unbalanced.
  • Knee-length kurti: Palazzo, straight pants, churidar or jeans all work.
  • Long kurti (below the knee): Palazzo, churidar or slim pants. Keep the bottom fitted if the kurti is flared.

Match by occasion

  • Daily and college: Cotton palazzo or leggings. Comfortable and easy to wash.
  • Office: Cigarette pants or straight pants in solid colours. They look neat and professional.
  • Functions: Churidar, sharara or silk-blend palazzo that matches the kurti.

Fabric and fit

  • Leggings: Check that they're opaque and don't turn see-through when stretched. Ankle length is usually the most flattering.
  • Palazzo: Cotton or rayon drape well. Check the length so it doesn't drag on the floor.
  • Pants: Pick a comfortable waist, ideally with elastic at the back.
  • Size: Bottoms often run smaller than tops, so check the waist and hip separately.

Common mistakes

  • Wearing palazzo with a very short kurti, which makes the look uneven.
  • Buying thin leggings that become see-through.
  • Not checking the waist and hip measurements of bottoms separately.

Frequently asked questions

Which bottom is most versatile?

Straight pants work with almost every kurti, from casual to office.

Can I wear leggings with any kurti?

Best with kurtis that cover the hip. Avoid very short or thin kurtis with leggings.

How do I stop palazzo from dragging?

Check the length in the listing and compare it with your height, or hem it once it arrives.
