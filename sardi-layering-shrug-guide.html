Sardi mein layering: shrug, sweater aur stole se look

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Dilli-NCR jaisi jagah mein sardi mein sirf jacket pehenna kaafi nahi hota. Layering se aap garam bhi rehte ho aur look bhi achha lagta hai.

Layering ke 4 tarike

  • Lamba shrug: Kurta ya dress ke upar. Ethnic aur western dono ke saath chalta hai.
  • Sweater ya sweatshirt: Palazzo ya jeans ke saath casual look ke liye.
  • Stole ya shawl: Kurta ya saree ke upar, function mein bhi chal jaata hai.
  • Jacket: Kurti ya dress ke upar, outing ke liye.

Fabric ka dhyan

Wool blend zyada garam hota hai par mehenga hota hai. Acrylic sasta aur halka hota hai, par kabhi-kabhi chubhta hai ya static banata hai, to andar naram inner pehen lo. Listing mein fabric ki percentage zaroor dekho.

Rang

Gehre rang (maroon, navy, bottle green, grey) sardi mein achhe lagte hain aur dhool-mitti bhi kam dikhate hain. Ek hi layer par bright rang rakho, baaki neutral.
