College ke liye kurti ₹500 ke andar: kya dekhein

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Roz college jaana ho to kurti aaramdayak, dhone mein tikau aur sasti honi chahiye. ₹500 mein ye teeno cheezein mil sakti hain, bas fabric aur reviews sahi dekhne padte hain.

₹500 mein kya mil sakta hai

Is budget mein simple cotton ya rayon ki printed kurti milti hai. Bhaari embroidery ya zari kaam ki ummeed mat rakho, wo is price par aksar kamzor hota hai.

Dekhne wali 4 cheezein

  • Fabric: Cotton roz ke liye sabse aaramdayak hai. Rayon naram hota hai par pehli dhulai mein sikud sakta hai, to dhone ke instructions padh lo.
  • Size: Cotton dhone ke baad thoda sikud sakta hai. Do size ke beech mein ho to bada size lo.
  • Rang nikalna: Reviews mein dekho ki dhone par rang jaata hai ya nahi. Gehre rang aur bade print par ye shikayat zyada hoti hai.
  • Lambai aur slit: Chalne, seedhi chadhne aur bike par baithne mein aaram wali lambai chuno. Bahut tang ya bahut lambi kurti college mein dikkat deti hai.

Ek kurti, teen look

  • Jeans ke saath: Straight kurti ko jeans aur sneakers ke saath pehno. Casual aur aaramdayak.
  • Palazzo ya leggings ke saath: Classroom ya presentation ke liye simple aur saaf look.
  • Jacket ya shrug ke saath: Sardi mein upar se denim jacket ya lamba shrug daal do, kurti wahi rahegi.
