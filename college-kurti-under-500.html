Kurti Under ₹500 for College: What to Look For

Updated November 2026

If you go to college every day, a kurti should be comfortable, last through washes and be cheap. You can get all three for ₹500, as long as you check the fabric and reviews properly.

What you can get for ₹500

At this budget you get simple cotton or rayon printed kurtis. Don't expect heavy embroidery or zari work; it is usually weak at this price.

4 things to check

  • Fabric: Cotton is the most comfortable for daily wear. Rayon is soft but can shrink in the first wash, so read the care instructions.
  • Size: Cotton can shrink a little after washing. If you're between two sizes, take the bigger one.
  • Colour fading: Check in reviews whether the colour runs in the wash. Dark colours and large prints get this complaint more often.
  • Length and slit: Pick a length that's comfortable for walking, climbing stairs and sitting on a bike. A kurti that's too tight or too long is a hassle in college.

One kurti, three looks

  • With jeans: Wear a straight kurti with jeans and sneakers. Casual and comfortable.
  • With palazzo or leggings: A simple, clean look for class or presentations.
  • With a jacket or shrug: In winter, add a denim jacket or long shrug on top; the kurti stays the same.

Common mistakes

  • Buying from the photo alone. Look at reviews with customers' own photos.
  • Picking a very cheap 'combo' with no track record on quality. Check the seller's rating and reviews first.
  • Ignoring the size chart. Every brand's sizing is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quality of a ₹500 kurti like?

Mostly simple cotton or rayon. You can only judge stitching and colour fastness from reviews, so do read them.

Which fabric is right for daily wear?

Cotton, because it's breathable and easy to wash.

How big a size should I take?

If you're between two sizes and the fabric is cotton, take the bigger size. Measuring against the size chart is the most reliable way.
