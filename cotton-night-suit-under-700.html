Cotton Night Suit Under ₹700: Fabric, Fit and Comfort

Updated November 2026

A night suit is something you wear for hours every day, so comfort matters more than a fancy print. Under ₹700 there are good cotton sets if you know what to look for.

This guide covers fabric, fit and the small details that decide comfort.

Fabric

  • 100% cotton: Breathable and soft, good for most of the year. Can shrink a little on first wash.
  • Cotton blend: Shrinks less and holds shape, but is a bit less breathable.
  • Hosiery or knit cotton: Stretchy and soft. Good for the cooler months.
  • Satin-look: Looks smart but can feel hot and sticky in summer.

Fit and details

  • Size: Choose slightly loose for comfort, and check the chest and hip measurements. If you plan to size up for room, check the length too.
  • Waistband: Soft elastic or drawstring is more comfortable than a tight band. Check it doesn't dig in.
  • Pockets: A small pocket is handy for a phone.
  • Shrinkage: Expect a little shrinkage in cotton, so avoid choosing the tightest fit.

Care

Wash in cold water and dry in shade to protect print and fabric. Check customer photos after a few washes to see how the colour holds.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a tight fit that will feel smaller after washing.
  • Buying satin-look fabric for hot weather.
  • Ignoring the waistband type, which affects comfort all night.

Frequently asked questions

Cotton or blend for night suits?

100% cotton is breathable and soft. A blend shrinks less. Choose by season and how you wash.

Will it shrink?

Cotton can shrink slightly on the first wash, so don't pick the tightest size.

What size should I take?

A little loose is usually most comfortable. Check chest and hip measurements.
