Oxidised Jewellery Under ₹500: What to Buy and How to Care

Updated November 2026

Oxidised jewellery goes well with cotton kurtis, sarees and western outfits, and it's affordable. The catch is that quality varies a lot at this price.

Here is what to check and how to look after it so it lasts.

What to check before you order

  • Material: Look for the base metal in the description. Brass and German silver base last longer than very cheap alloys.
  • Skin sensitivity: If you get itching or rashes with earrings, look for nickel-free or hypoallergenic mentions, and check reviews.
  • Weight: Large earrings can be heavy. Check the weight or customer photos before ordering.
  • Finish: Look at customer photos for how the black finish holds, as it can peel in cheap pieces.
  • What's included: Check whether it's a set (necklace, earrings) or a single piece.

How to pair it

  • Cotton and printed kurtis: Medium or large earrings and a simple necklace.
  • Sarees: Statement earrings or a choker with a plain saree.
  • Western wear: A single bold pair of earrings or a long pendant.
  • Heavy outfits: Keep jewellery small. Let the outfit do the talking.

Care

Keep it dry, away from perfume and water. Store each piece in a small pouch or box so pieces don't scratch each other. Wipe with a soft cloth after wearing.

Common mistakes

  • Wearing it while bathing or in the rain, which wears off the finish.
  • Choosing heavy earrings without checking the weight.
  • Ignoring skin sensitivity and then not being able to wear it.

Frequently asked questions

Does oxidised jewellery cause allergies?

Some people react to certain metals. Look for nickel-free mentions and check reviews from sensitive-skin buyers.

How can I make it last?

Keep it dry, wipe after wearing and store in a pouch away from perfume.

Is it good for daily wear?

Light pieces work for daily wear, but avoid wearing it while bathing or sweating heavily.
