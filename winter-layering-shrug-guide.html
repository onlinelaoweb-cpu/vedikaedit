Winter Layering: Looks with a Shrug, Sweater and Stole

Updated November 2026

In a place like Delhi-NCR, just wearing a jacket isn't enough in winter. Layering keeps you warm and looks good too.

4 ways to layer

  • Long shrug: Over a kurta or dress. Works with both ethnic and western.
  • Sweater or sweatshirt: With palazzo or jeans for a casual look.
  • Stole or shawl: Over a kurta or saree; it works at functions too.
  • Jacket: Over a kurti or dress, for outings.

Mind the fabric

Wool blend is warmer but costs more. Acrylic is cheap and light, but can itch or create static, so wear a soft inner underneath. Always check the fabric percentage in the listing.

Colour

Deep colours (maroon, navy, bottle green, grey) look good in winter and show less dust. Keep a bright colour on one layer and the rest neutral.

Common mistakes

  • Wearing one thick piece instead of thin layers. Thin layers stacked on each other keep you warmer.
  • Buying without checking the fabric details.
  • Buying a shrug that's too small, so the kurta underneath sticks out.

Frequently asked questions

Which shrug looks good over a kurti?

A long shrug that's slightly shorter than, or the same length as, the kurti. Contrast or matching colours both work.

What's cheap and warm?

Wool blend is warmer; acrylic is cheaper. A thermal inner is a cheap and useful option.

Can I wear a shawl at a function?

Yes, a good stole or shawl looks good with an ethnic outfit.
