Online fashion kharidte waqt 7 galtiyan

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Online kapde kharidna aasan hai, par size aur quality ki galti mehengi pad sakti hai. Ye 7 galtiyan sabse aam hain, aur inse bachna mushkil nahi.

7 aam galtiyan

  • Size chart na dekhna: Har brand ka S, M, L alag hota hai. Apna ek fit kapda napo aur chart se milao.
  • Fabric na padhna: Listing mein fabric ka naam aur dhone ke instructions padho. Rayon sikud sakta hai, polyester garmi mein chipchipa lagta hai.
  • Sirf photo dekhna: Reviews mein customers ki apni photo wale reviews dekho. Model ki photo mein editing aur lighting hoti hai.
  • Discount ka percent dekhna: MRP bahut oonchi likhi ho sakti hai. Asli price ko dusre sellers ya listings se compare karo.
  • Return policy na dekhna: Category mein return ya exchange ka window dekho.
  • Seller ko na dekhna: 'Sold by' wala naam aur seller ki rating dekho.
  • Last minute order karna: Function se kam se kam ek hafta pehle mangao, taaki size badalna pade to waqt ho.

Rang ka dhokha

Screen par rang thoda alag dikh sakta hai. Reviews ki photos mein asli rang dekho, aur agar rang bahut zaroori hai (jaise shaadi ke liye) to dhyan se match karo.
