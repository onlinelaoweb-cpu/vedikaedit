Jhumke kaise chunein: outfit aur chehre ke hisaab se

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Ek achhe jhumke poore outfit ko badal dete hain. Par galat size ya bhaari jhumke kaan mein dard kar sakte hain, isliye chunte waqt kuch baatein dhyan rakhni chahiye.

Outfit ke saath match

  • Bhaari outfit: Zari ya embroidery wale kapdon ke saath chhote ya medium jhumke chuno.
  • Simple outfit: Plain kurta ya cotton saree ke saath bade jhumke look poora karte hain.
  • Silver ya oxidised: Cotton, denim aur casual kurti ke saath achhe lagte hain.
  • Gold-tone: Silk aur embroidery wale kapdon ke saath khilte hain.

Chehre ke hisaab se (aam salah, koi pakka rule nahi)

  • Gol chehra: Lambe jhumke chehre ko lamba dikhate hain.
  • Chaukor chehra: Gol ya curved jhumke kono ko naram dikhate hain.
  • Lamba chehra: Chhote ya medium jhumke ya studs achhe lagte hain.

Aaram aur dekhbhaal

  • Bhaari jhumke kaan ko kheench sakte hain. Lambe time ke liye halke jhumke chuno.
  • Agar artificial jewellery se kaan mein jalan ya khujli hoti hai to listing mein 'nickel free' ya 'hypoallergenic' dekho.
  • Jewellery ko pani, perfume aur cream se door rakho, aur alag dibbi mein rakho.
