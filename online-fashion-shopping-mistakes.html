7 Mistakes to Avoid When Buying Fashion Online

Updated November 2026

Buying clothes online is easy, but a size or quality mistake can be costly. These 7 mistakes are the most common, and avoiding them isn't hard.

7 common mistakes

  • Not checking the size chart: Every brand's S, M, L is different. Measure a piece that fits you and compare it with the chart.
  • Not reading the fabric: Read the fabric name and care instructions in the listing. Rayon can shrink, and polyester feels sticky in summer.
  • Looking only at the photo: Look for reviews with customers' own photos. Model photos involve editing and lighting.
  • Going by the discount percent: The MRP can be listed very high. Compare the real price with other sellers or listings.
  • Skipping the return policy: Check the return or exchange window for that category.
  • Ignoring the seller: Check the 'Sold by' name and the seller's rating.
  • Ordering at the last minute: Order at least a week before the function, so there's time to exchange if the size is wrong.

The colour trap

Colours can look a little different on screen. Check the real colour in review photos, and if the colour matters a lot (say, for a wedding), match it carefully.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use a size chart?

Measure a piece of clothing that fits you well (chest, waist, length) and compare it with the listing's chart.

Can I trust discounts?

Look at the real price more than the discount percent, and compare with other sellers.

What should I check about returns?

How many days the return window is and what conditions apply.
