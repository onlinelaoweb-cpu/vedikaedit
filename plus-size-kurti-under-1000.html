Plus-Size Kurti Under ₹1,000: Fit, Fabric and What to Check

Updated November 2026

Finding a kurti that fits well in larger sizes takes a bit more care, because the label alone doesn't tell you much. Under ₹1,000 there are good options, but it pays to read the chart closely.

These are the checks that help most, along with the cuts that tend to be comfortable.

Read the size chart carefully

  • Body or garment: Check whether the chart lists your body measurements or the kurti's own measurements. Garment measurements should be a little larger than you.
  • Chest, waist and hip: Compare all three, not just the chest. Hip room matters for A-line and straight cuts.
  • Length and sleeve: Check the length and the sleeve opening, which can be tight around the upper arm.
  • Brand to brand: XXL or 3XL differ between brands. Use your measurements, not just the label.

Cuts that are comfortable

  • A-line: Skims over the hip and is easy to move in. Works for most people.
  • Straight cut with slit: Clean look. Choose a little extra ease so it doesn't pull.
  • Anarkali or flared: Fitted at the top and wide at the bottom. Check the chest and arm measurements.
  • Kaftan or loose cut: Maximum comfort for hot weather and travel.

Fabric

Cotton and cotton-blend with a little stretch are comfortable. Rayon drapes well but can be see-through in light colours. Check customer photos at larger sizes, since many reviews are on smaller ones.

Common mistakes

  • Ordering by the label alone without checking the garment measurement.
  • Ignoring sleeve and hip measurements, which cause most fit complaints.
  • Skipping reviews from buyers of the same size.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find the right size?

Measure your chest, waist and hip, then match them with the chart's garment measurements, aiming for a little ease.

Which cut is most comfortable?

A-line and kaftan cuts are generally the easiest. Check the specific listing's measurements.

Should I size up?

If you're between sizes or the fabric doesn't stretch, going up is safer.
