Daily Wear Cotton Saree Under ₹1,500: How to Choose

Updated November 2026

A good cotton saree is comfortable, breathable and gets better with washing. Under ₹1,500 you can find solid choices, but 'cotton' on a listing can mean very different things.

This guide explains what to look for so the saree you receive matches what you expected.

Types of cotton sarees

  • Handloom cotton: Textured, breathable, with visible weave. Good for office and everyday wear. Slight irregularities in weave are normal.
  • Cotton silk: A cotton and silk-look blend with a little sheen. Better for functions than for daily wear.
  • Printed cotton: Light and easy to maintain, good for summer. Check whether the print is washable.
  • Mul or voile cotton: Very soft and light, but can be see-through, so check for lining or wear a proper petticoat.

Check before you order

  • Blouse piece: See whether it's included and its length. Most listings give about 0.8-1 metre, which can be tight for larger sizes.
  • Saree length: A standard saree is about 5.5 metres. Shorter ones are harder to drape.
  • Fabric weight: Reviews will tell you whether it feels stiff or soft after washing.
  • Colour fastness: Dark colours can bleed in the first wash. Wash separately in cold water the first time.
  • Ready-to-wear option: If draping is a hurdle, a pre-stitched saree saves time. Check the waist size in the chart.

Care tips

Starching makes cotton crisp for office wear. Wash in cold water, dry in shade to protect the colour, and iron while slightly damp.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming 'cotton' means 100% cotton. Read the material line in the listing.
  • Not checking the blouse piece length before planning stitching.
  • Washing a dark saree with other clothes the first time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know a saree is real cotton?

Read the fabric line for the percentage, and look at customer photos. Pure cotton feels slightly rough when new and softens with washing.

Which cotton saree is best for office?

Handloom or printed cotton in solid, muted colours drapes neatly and needs little maintenance.

Do I have to stitch the blouse?

Not always. Readymade blouses are available in standard sizes, but a tailored blouse fits better.
