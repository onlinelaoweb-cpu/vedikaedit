Daily Wear Flats and Sandals Under ₹800: What to Check

Updated November 2026

Footwear is the item people return most for fit, so a little care before ordering saves a lot of trouble. Under ₹800 you can find comfortable daily pairs if you check the sole, straps and size.

This guide covers what to check for flats, sandals and slip-ons.

Sole and cushioning

  • Sole material: EVA and rubber soles are light and flexible. Hard PVC soles feel stiff and can slip on wet floors.
  • Cushioning: Look for a padded footbed if you stand or walk a lot. A completely flat, thin sole tires the feet by evening.
  • Grip: Textured soles help on wet surfaces. Check the sole photo, not only the top view.

Fit and straps

  • Size: Measure your foot length in cm and compare it with the brand's chart. If you're between sizes, go up, especially for closed shoes.
  • Straps: Adjustable straps give flexibility on wide feet. Thin straps can dig in after a few hours.
  • Toe box: Pointed flats look sharp but can pinch. Round or square toes are more comfortable for daily use.
  • Break-in: Wear new footwear at home for an hour first to check for pressure points before using it outdoors.

Material

Synthetic leather is common at this price. It cleans easily but doesn't breathe, so socks or thin liners help in summer. Fabric and canvas uppers breathe better but need regular washing.

Common mistakes

  • Ordering by your usual size without checking the brand's size chart.
  • Choosing a very thin sole for a long walking day.
  • Wearing new footwear for a full day without a short trial first.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get comfortable footwear under ₹800?

Yes, if you check the sole, fit and reviews that mention walking comfort. Photos in reviews help you judge real quality.

What if I'm between two sizes?

Take the larger size for closed shoes. For adjustable-strap sandals, your regular size may work.

How do I keep new footwear from biting?

Wear it indoors for short sessions first, and use a soft liner or thin socks if the strap rubs.
