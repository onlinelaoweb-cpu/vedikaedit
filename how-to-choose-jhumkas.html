How to Choose Jhumkas: By Outfit and Face Shape

Updated November 2026

A good pair of jhumkas can transform a whole outfit. But the wrong size or heavy jhumkas can make your ears hurt, so keep a few things in mind when choosing.

Matching your outfit

  • Heavy outfit: With zari or embroidered clothes, choose small or medium jhumkas.
  • Simple outfit: Big jhumkas complete a plain kurta or cotton saree.
  • Silver or oxidised: Look good with cotton, denim and casual kurtis.
  • Gold-tone: Suits silk and embroidered clothes.

By face shape (general advice, not a firm rule)

  • Round face: Long jhumkas make the face look longer.
  • Square face: Round or curved jhumkas soften the corners.
  • Long face: Small or medium jhumkas or studs look good.

Comfort and care

  • Heavy jhumkas can pull on the ear. Choose lightweight ones for long hours.
  • If artificial jewellery causes irritation or itching, look for 'nickel free' or 'hypoallergenic' in the listing.
  • Keep jewellery away from water, perfume and creams, and store it in a separate box.

Frequently asked questions

How big should my jhumkas be?

It depends on the outfit. Small with a heavy outfit, bigger with a simple one.

How do I keep artificial jewellery safe for my ears?

Look for nickel-free or hypoallergenic options, and take them off right away if you feel irritation.

How should I store jewellery?

Dry, in a separate box, away from water and perfume.
