Kurta Set With Dupatta Under ₹1,500: College, Office and Festive

Updated November 2026

A kurta set with dupatta is the most practical ethnic purchase: top, bottom and dupatta come together, so you don't have to match pieces. Under ₹1,500 you can find sets that work for college, office and small functions.

Here is how to pick one that fits well, lasts and can be styled in more than one way.

Pick by where you'll wear it

  • College: Printed cotton kurta with palazzo or straight pants. Comfortable and easy to wash.
  • Office: Solid or small-print rayon or cotton, straight cut with pants. Choose muted colours.
  • Small functions: Georgette or silk-blend with light embroidery or gota work. Add jhumkas and juttis to dress it up.

Check before you order

  • What's included: Confirm that the dupatta and bottom are part of the set. Many listings show them in photos but sell the kurta alone.
  • Size chart: Compare chest, waist and hip with the chart. Bottoms often run smaller than tops, so check them separately.
  • Fabric: Rayon is soft and falls well but wrinkles and can shrink. Cotton is easy to care for. Polyester blends hold their shape but feel warm in summer.
  • Dupatta quality: Check its length and material in the description. Thin, short dupattas make the set look cheaper.
  • Reviews with photos: See real customer photos to judge colour and fit, and read comments about the first wash.

Styling in more than one way

Wear the dupatta draped for functions and skip it for college. Pair the kurta with jeans on another day. Change the jewellery and footwear, and the same set gives you two or three different looks.

Common mistakes

  • Not checking whether the dupatta is included.
  • Using a single size label for both top and bottom without comparing measurements.
  • Buying only on colour from the photo and ignoring the fabric.

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear a kurta set to the office?

Yes. Choose solid or small prints in muted colours and a straight cut with pants. It usually works for most offices, but follow your workplace's dress code.

Cotton or rayon?

Cotton is easier to care for and lasts well. Rayon feels softer and falls nicely but needs gentle washing.

How do I make one set look different?

Change the dupatta style, switch between palazzo and pants, and vary jewellery and footwear.
