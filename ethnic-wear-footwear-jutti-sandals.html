Ethnic wear ke saath footwear: jutti ya sandal?

Nov 2026 mein update kiya gaya

Ethnic outfit ke saath galat footwear poora look bigaad deta hai, aur der tak pehenna ho to paon bhi dard karte hain. Ye guide aaram aur style dono ka dhyan rakhta hai.

Kaun sa outfit, kaun sa footwear

  • Kurta set ya anarkali: Jutti ya flats.
  • Saree: Block heels ya wedges, jisse lambai bhi theek dikhe.
  • Lehenga ya sharara: Jutti ya heels. Lehenge ki lambai se footwear zyada nahi dikhta.
  • Roz ki kurti: Kolhapuri chappal ya flats.

Jutti ke baare mein

Naye jutti shuru mein thode tang lag sakte hain. Function se pehle ghar par kuch ghante pehen ke dekh lo, taaki chhaale na padein.

Size aur material

  • Synthetic material: Ye aksar khichta nahi hai, to size seedha sahi lo.
  • Do size ke beech mein: Bada size lo aur pair mein soft insole laga lo.
  • Lambe function: Ek extra flats ka pair saath rakho.
