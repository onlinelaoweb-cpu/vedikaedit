About The Vedika Edit

What The Vedika Edit is

The Vedika Edit is a fashion guide site that helps you choose clothes, jewellery and accessories to fit your budget. The guides here are written around everyday shopping questions: what to check, what to avoid and how to split your budget.

Where the links go

Most links on the site open Amazon.in search results. We don't guarantee the price, stock or quality of any product, because that depends on Amazon and its sellers.

Affiliate disclosure

The Vedika Edit is part of the Amazon Associates program. When you buy something through our links we earn a small commission, and you pay nothing extra. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

How much to trust our advice

The budgets and tips in the guides are general advice, not a recommendation of any particular product. Check the size chart, reviews and return policy yourself before you buy.

Languages

We write the site ourselves in English and Hindi. The other Indian languages in the language menu are translated automatically by Google Translate, so the wording may not be perfect. When in doubt, refer to the English or Hindi version.
